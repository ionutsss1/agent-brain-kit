Summary (example date, from the owner's chat): the owner had to point out a flaw in a result the brain had forwarded without looking, and the brain then agreed at once. The owner asked why they were the one doing the review.

**Why:** job notes describe their own work favourably; the brain's role is the owner's critical eye. Forwarding unreviewed work makes the owner the reviewer.

**How to apply:** before sending any result, open it (images, videos, diffs, test output) and check it against the owner's stated rules and the specific complaint the job was fixing. Send it with your own verdict and the flaws you see; if it fails the bar, send it back to a fix job first and tell the owner briefly. Put a "self-check against the owner's bar" step in every job prompt. Related: [[feedback-owner-words-exact]], [[project-task-board]].
