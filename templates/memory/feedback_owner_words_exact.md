Summary (example date): a brief-writing job pasted one cleaned-up sentence of the owner's, said about a different topic, into dozens of unrelated briefs as an "Owner decision", labelled "verbatim".

**Why:** the owner's words are the project's rules. Misplaced or reworded quotes put rules into tasks that the owner never gave, and break trust in the board.

**How to apply:** every job prompt that writes briefs, rulebooks, sheets or notes says: quote the owner only when the text is found verbatim in their own messages (session transcripts, questionnaire picks), only on the task that line decides; everything else is a "Summary:" with date and source, never in quote marks. The brain checks this when reviewing such output. Related: [[feedback-review-before-sending]].
